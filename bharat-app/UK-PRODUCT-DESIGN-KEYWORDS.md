## USAGE GUIDANCE

**Only use a phrase when BOTH are true:**

1. It appears meaningfully in real current UK job descriptions (documented above)
2. Sorted genuinely demonstrates it in the case study

**Phrases NOT to insert despite relevance:**

- **"design systems"** (as a primary skill) — Sorted shows shared patterns but does not present a standalone design system project. Use "reusable patterns" or "shared components" instead.
- **"agentic"** — Sorted's AI is assistive/conversational, not truly agentic. Do not claim agentic capability.
- **"prototyping in code"** — The case study does not demonstrate this.
- **"user research" (as conducted)** — Baymard research is referenced but original user research is not claimed. Do not inflate.
- **"shipped"** — Sorted is concept work. Use "designed" or "explored" instead.
- **"regulated environments"** — Not demonstrated.

**Phrases safe to use naturally:**

- end-to-end
- systems thinking
- cross-domain
- AI-powered
- conversational
- native mobile
- commerce
- marketplace
- payments / checkout
- 0→1 / zero-to-one
- complex problem spaces
- information architecture

- shared infrastructure

## OBSERVATIONS

1. **"Product Designer" is the dominant title** across all tiers. "UX Designer" is declining except at Google. The case study should use "Product Designer" language.

2. **AI is now table-stakes language** — ~70% of roles mention AI in some capacity, even if not AI-specific roles. Most commonly: "use AI tools in your workflow" and "design AI-powered experiences."

3. **"Ambiguity" is the most common soft-skill signal** — appearing in ~65% of senior+ JDs. Sorted's concept nature is actually a strength here.

4. **Commerce + Payments + Marketplace** form a cluster — many UK roles sit at this intersection (Wise, Checkout, Deliveroo, ASOS, Lyst, Apron). Sorted touches all three.

5. **"Shipped" is critical vocabulary** — but Sorted hasn't shipped. The case study must navigate this honestly. Use "designed," "explored," "proposed" — never "shipped" or "launched."

6. **Native mobile** is increasingly specified over "responsive web" — Ticketmaster, ASOS, Monzo all explicitly state "mobile-first responsive work is not a substitute" for native experience.

7. **Salary range context**: Senior Product Designer roles in London range £75k–£120k. Staff/Principal roles range £93k–£161k (Microsoft top end). Concept: Sorted should signal senior-level thinking.
