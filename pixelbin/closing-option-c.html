Option C — Narrative Arc

Explored

14 Flows,
170 Frames

V1 explored everything. AI Influencer, Script to Video, Batch Processing, Social Media Campaign, Upload and Chat Edit — fourteen flows mapped across 170 prototype frames. The canvas paradigm was the bet: what if every generated asset lived on a shared spatial workspace instead of disappearing into a download folder? The first version tested that bet in every direction at once.

Shipped

8 Flows,
57 Components

V2 cut ruthlessly. Nine flows were removed. The surviving five mapped to the core creative loop: generate, edit, upload, batch, campaign. Engineering then expanded it to eight — not because design added features, but because video generation needed to split into three separate model endpoints. The design stayed unified. The engineering became specific. Core generation and editing flows are live. Campaign planning and batch mode are shipping next. I was the sole designer on the POC.

Learned

Orchestration
> Generation

The product bet paid off. The differentiator was never image quality — every model produces good images now. The differentiator was everything around it: the AI that analyses before the user asks, the campaign planner that coordinates seven assets from a single prompt, the batch processor that applies one edit across a catalogue. The future of AI creative tools isn't better generation. It's better orchestration. The AI proposes. The user approves.
