Privacy and cookies

Last updated: 25 September 2026.

This is Terry John Paul’s design portfolio. For privacy enquiries, email terryjohnpaul20@gmail.com.

Optional analytics

Selected pages use Microsoft Clarity for heatmaps and session recordings and Vercel Web Analytics for traffic information. These scripts are not loaded until you accept optional analytics. You can reject them and still read the portfolio.

Clarity records interaction information such as clicks, scrolling and page layout. Its first-party cookies include _clck, which associates visits, and _clsk, which connects page views within a session. Advertising storage is denied by this site.

Microsoft describes standard recording retention as 30 days, with favourites and sampled recordings kept for up to nine months. Provider settings may affect retention. See Clarity’s retention information, its cookie information and the Microsoft privacy statement.

Vercel analytics reports visits and page performance context. It is also treated as optional here. See Vercel’s analytics privacy information. The account’s analytics retention setting has not been independently verified.

Your choices

Your choice is stored on this device under portfolio-privacy-v1 for 180 days. Optional analytics is off by default. Withdrawing consent clears Clarity cookies under this site’s control and reloads the page to stop its runtime. It cannot erase information already received by a provider or cookies belonging to another domain.

Portfolio features and prototypes

The optional terminal interface stores its theme, display preferences and introductory state on your device, and command history for the browser session. Browser settings can clear this storage. These values support the interface rather than advertising.

The Toneflix case study and its linked prototype do not run analytics. The prototype uses fictional data and simulated permissions. It does not connect to health services or submit the demonstration entries to a server.

Hosting and contact

Vercel serves the portfolio and Cloudflare serves hosted media. Requests necessarily include network information such as an IP address. Hosting security logs and provider retention are governed by their services; this page does not claim a verified account-specific retention period. See Vercel’s privacy policy and Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Email links open your email application. Social links take you to services with their own privacy policies. The portfolio has no public contact-form submission endpoint.
