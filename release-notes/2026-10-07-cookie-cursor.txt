Fixes the missing mouse cursor when Cookie preferences is open.

The homepage hides the system cursor and draws a custom cursor in the page. Native modal dialogs render in the browser's top layer, above that custom cursor, leaving visitors without a visible pointer.

- Restore the native cursor within the privacy dialog and its backdrop.
- Show a pointer over buttons, links, the analytics checkbox and its full label.
- Hide the page's custom cursor while the modal is open; restore it on close.
- Extend the homepage regression check to cover modal cursor styles and restoration.

This is a scoped shared-CSS fix. Consent choices, analytics loading and privacy text are unchanged.
